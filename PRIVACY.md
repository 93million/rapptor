# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Insert Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how **Rapptor**, developed by **93 Million** (“we,” “our,” or “us”), handles your data. By using Rapptor, you agree to the practices described below.

---

## 1. Overview
Rapptor is self-hosted VPN software that runs entirely on a hosting service you purchase separately. All data generated by your use of Rapptor remains on your server unless you choose to share it.

---

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect
- We do **not** log or record your online activity when you access Rapptor through the web interface or the WireGuard-based VPN app.
- We do **not** track your IP addresses, browsing history, DNS requests, or any other activity-related data.
- We do **not** collect telemetry or usage statistics from your Rapptor instance.
- We do **not** send any data from your instance to our servers or any third-party tracking systems.

---

## 3. Error Logs
- Error logs generated by the Rapptor web interface are stored **in memory only** on your server.
- They are used solely for debugging technical issues.
- They do **not** contain personally identifiable information such as IP addresses.
- They are not written to disk and never leave your server.

---

## 4. Third-Party Applications
If you install third-party applications (e.g., Plex) on your server alongside Rapptor, those applications may generate logs or enable telemetry. These features are not controlled by Rapptor. If you wish to avoid such data collection, you should research and disable these features.

---

## 5. Hosting Provider Data
Since Rapptor runs on a hosting provider you choose, your hosting provider may have its own privacy practices. We are not responsible for your hosting provider’s handling of data. Please review your hosting provider’s privacy policy separately.

---

## 6. Support Requests
If you require technical support for Rapptor, we may ask you to share relevant non-sensitive information voluntarily (e.g., screenshots of errors). Any information you share with us through [https://github.com/rapptorvpn/rapptor/issues](https://github.com/rapptorvpn/rapptor/issues) will be publicly visible on GitHub unless you submit it via a private channel.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a revised “Last Updated” date. Continued use of Rapptor after updates indicates your acceptance of the changes.
